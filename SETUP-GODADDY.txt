FLUSHSTAR WEBSITE + ADMIN PANEL — SETUP ON GODADDY
==================================================

This is the website plus a visitor tracking system and an admin panel to view
it. Everything runs on your own GoDaddy hosting. No other service, no monthly
fee, nothing sending your visitors' data to anyone else.

It needs a Linux / cPanel hosting plan, which gives you PHP and MySQL. That is
GoDaddy's normal "Web Hosting". It will NOT work on Website Builder, which
cannot run this kind of code. If you are unsure, open your hosting dashboard —
if you can see cPanel, you are fine.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 1 — BACK UP WHAT IS THERE NOW
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
In File Manager, open public_html, select everything, Compress to a zip, and
download it. Do not skip this. It is how you undo everything in two minutes.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2 — MAKE THE DATABASE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
In cPanel, open "MySQL Database Wizard".

  1. Database name: flushstar          → Next Step
  2. Username: fsadmin
     Password: use the Generate button, and COPY IT SOMEWHERE FIRST
                                         → Create User
  3. Tick "ALL PRIVILEGES"              → Next Step

GoDaddy adds your account name to the front of both, so you will end up with
something like "abc123_flushstar" and "abc123_fsadmin". Write down all three:
the full database name, the full username, and the password.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 3 — FILL IN THE SETTINGS FILE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Open admin/config.php on your computer in Notepad or TextEdit. Replace the
four REPLACE_WITH_... values:

  db_name  the full database name from step 2
  db_user  the full username from step 2
  db_pass  the password from step 2
  salt     any long line of random letters and numbers you make up

Leave db_host as 'localhost'. Save the file.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 4 — UPLOAD
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. In File Manager, go into public_html.
2. Delete the old website files. Leave alone anything that is not the website,
   especially .well-known, cgi-bin, and any mail folders.
3. Upload flushstar-site.zip, then right-click it and choose Extract.
4. The extract creates a folder. Open it, select everything inside, and Move it
   up one level into public_html. Then delete the empty folder and the zip.

public_html should end up containing:
  index.html   about.html   dealer-apply.php   style.css   og-image.jpg
  track.php    img/         admin/             dealer/


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 5 — CREATE YOUR LOGIN
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
In a browser go to:

  https://www.flushstarmarine.com/admin/setup.php

It builds the database tables and asks you to pick a username and password.
Use a real password, at least 10 characters — this page is on the open
internet.

If it says "Database connection failed", the details in config.php do not match
step 2. That is the only thing that error means.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 6 — DELETE THE SETUP FILE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Back in File Manager, delete admin/setup.php.

It refuses to create a second account once one exists, so it is not a hole, but
there is no reason to leave it lying around.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DONE — USING IT
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  https://www.flushstarmarine.com/admin/

Sign in with what you created. You get:

  Visitors and page views, for today / 7 / 30 / 90 days
  A traffic chart by day
  Where people came from — Google, Facebook, Boat Trader, direct, and so on
  Which pages they looked at
  Phone versus desktop, and which browsers
  A live list of recent visits

There are four tabs across the top: Visitors, Orders, Dealers and Products.
The last three are the dealer portal, covered below.

Visit the site yourself in another browser and refresh the panel — you will see
yourself appear.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A NOTE ON PRIVACY, BECAUSE IT MATTERS FOR A BUSINESS SITE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This does NOT store IP addresses. The address is scrambled together with your
salt and the date into an anonymous daily id, which is enough to count unique
visitors and cannot be turned back into an address or a person.

That is a deliberate choice. It keeps the numbers useful while meaning you are
not holding personal data you would then be responsible for. It also means the
panel cannot tell you WHO an individual visitor is — only how many, from where,
on what. If someone ever asks for named visitors, that is a different thing
entirely and worth talking about first.

Known bots and crawlers are ignored, so the numbers are real people.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE DEALER PORTAL
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Boat dealers sign in and send you orders. No card is taken and nothing is
charged — an order arrives, you confirm it, and you invoice the way you do now.
It installs with the rest of the site. There is nothing extra to set up.

PRICES ARE OFF TO START WITH
  You asked to hold prices back, so the portal ships with them hidden. Dealers
  send quantities only. When you are ready, go to Products, tick "Dealers can
  see prices and order totals", and fill the price column in. If you turn
  prices on while some products have no price, the panel tells you which.

HOW A DEALER GETS AN ACCOUNT
  1. They find www.flushstarmarine.com/dealer-apply.php and apply. It appears
     in your admin panel under Dealers with a blue number on the tab.
  2. You press "Set up account". Their details are already filled in; you pick
     the username they will sign in with.
  3. The panel shows a temporary password ONCE. Read it to them over the phone.
     It is not stored anywhere readable — not even you can look it up later.
  4. On their first sign-in they must choose their own password before they can
     do anything else.

  You can also create an account directly, without an application. Same form.

WHEN AN ORDER COMES IN
  The Orders tab shows a count of new ones. Open an order and you get the
  dealer, their phone, what they want, where to ship it and any note. Set the
  status — Confirmed, Invoiced, Shipped or Cancelled — and you can type a
  message the dealer sees on their own order page.

  Nothing is emailed. Changing a status does not notify anybody. The dealer
  sees it next time they look. Call or invoice as you normally would.

IF A DEALER FORGETS THEIR PASSWORD
  Dealers tab, "Reset password" on their row. You get a new temporary password
  to read out. Their old one stops working immediately.

IF YOU STOP DEALING WITH SOMEONE
  "Disable" on their row. They cannot sign in, and if they are signed in at the
  time they are thrown out on their next click. Their order history is kept.

SIX WRONG PASSWORDS AND THEY ARE LOCKED OUT
  For fifteen minutes. Resetting their password clears it straight away.

WHAT THE PORTAL DOES NOT DO
  It does not take payment, calculate tax or freight, check stock, or send
  email. Those were all deliberate — say the word and any of them can be added.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Page loads but the panel shows zeros
  Visit the public site first — the counter only records the public pages, not
  the admin panel. Your own admin visits are deliberately not counted.

"Database connection failed"
  config.php does not match the database you made in step 2. Check for the
  account prefix on both the database name and the username.

Old site still showing
  Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R), and check there is no leftover
  index.php or index.htm in public_html — Apache serves those before index.html.

Anything else, send me a screenshot and I will tell you exactly what to click.
