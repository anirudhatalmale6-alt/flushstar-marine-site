3. THE ADMIN PANEL
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Sign in at /admin/ — four tabs across the top.

VISITORS   How many people came, which pages they read, where they came
           from (Google, Facebook, direct), phone versus desktop, and a
           live list of recent visits. Today / 7 / 30 / 90 days.

           No IP addresses are stored. Visitors are counted using an
           anonymous daily code, so the numbers are useful but nobody
           is holding personal data. Known bots are ignored.

ORDERS     Every order dealers send. A blue number appears on the tab
           when new ones arrive. Open an order to see the dealer, their
           phone, what they want, where it ships and any note. Set the
           status and leave a message the dealer sees on their own page.

DEALERS    Trade accounts, and applications waiting to be approved.

PRODUCTS   The item list dealers order from, and the price switch.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. HOW THE DEALER PORTAL WORKS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. A boat dealer finds the site and fills in Become a Dealer.
2. Their application appears in the Dealers tab with a blue number.
3. You press "Set up account". Their details are already filled in;
   you choose the username.
4. The panel shows a temporary password ONCE. Read it to them by
   phone. It is stored scrambled — nobody can look it up later.
5. First time they sign in they must choose their own password before
   they can do anything else.
6. They order by typing quantities. It lands in your Orders tab.

NOTHING IS CHARGED AND NO CARD IS TAKEN. An order is a request. You
confirm it and invoice exactly as you do now.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. EVERYDAY JOBS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
New dealer            Dealers tab, "Set up account"
Forgotten password    Dealers tab, "Reset password" on their row.
                      Read out the new one. Their old one stops working.
Stop dealing with one Dealers tab, "Disable". They cannot sign in, and
                      if signed in they are thrown out on the next click.
                      Their order history is kept.
Add or edit products  Products tab. Change names, descriptions, add new
                      items, or untick Live to hide one.
Turn prices on        Products tab, tick "Dealers can see prices", then
                      fill in the price column. The panel warns you if
                      any live product has no price.

Six wrong passwords locks a dealer out for fifteen minutes. Resetting
their password clears it immediately.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. WHAT THE SITE DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
No card payments. No tax or freight calculation. No stock levels.
No emails are sent by the website at all.

That last one is deliberate: shared hosting email fails silently, and a
trade enquiry vanishing is worse than a tab you check. Dealer
applications and order status changes appear in the panel, not an inbox.

Any of these can be added later.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. STILL RECOMMENDED
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
a) ORIGIN CERTIFICATE — do before handing out dealer logins.
   The connection between Cloudflare and the web server is currently
   unencrypted on its last leg. Fine for public pages, not ideal for a
   page where dealers type passwords. Fixed with a free Cloudflare
   certificate installed on the server. Ten minutes, no cost.

b) SPF RECORD — fixes business email landing in spam.
   Their domain tells the world to spam-folder unauthenticated mail,
   then omits one of the two authentication records. Add this in
   Cloudflare DNS:

        Type: TXT
        Name: @
        Value: v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all

   Also worth checking DKIM signing is switched on inside their Google
   admin console — the record exists but may never have been activated.

c) HOSTING TERM — move from monthly to annual and roughly halve it.

d) WIX — leave it running about a week as a fallback, then cancel.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
To put the old Wix site back, in Cloudflare DNS:

    A record   flushstarmarine.com  ->  185.230.63.107   (grey cloud)
    CNAME      www                  ->  pointing.wixdns.net (grey cloud)

That reverses everything in about five minutes. Do not change the MX or
any TXT record — those are the email and must stay exactly as they are.
