Since 1988

Building outboard flushing systems since 1988.

Since 1988, FlushStar has engineered flushing hardware trusted by boaters and marine mechanics who know exactly what salt water does to an unprotected cooling system.

What began as a simple direct-connect fitting has grown into the FlushStar flushing system, built to clear salt, sand and mineral buildup from your outboard's full cooling circuit in one push-button cycle. No ear muffs to align. No guesswork about whether the job got done.

Every unit is a 12VDC, Plug n Play system that requires no additional programming, is made in the U.S.A., and works with nothing more than a garden hose. There is no need to run the engine, which means no noise, no emissions and no pollution. It works whether the boat is in or out of the water — making flushing hassle-free, unattended and fast.
